## ❓ FAQ

### **How do I use the app?**
Enter the total amount and the number of people.  
The app instantly calculates the tip and splits the bill.

### **Is my data stored anywhere?**
No.  
All calculations run locally on your device.  
No personal data is collected or transmitted.

### **Is the app free?**
Yes, the app is free to download.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

### **The tip doesn’t update**
Try closing the app and reopening it.  
If the issue continues, please reach out with steps to reproduce the problem.

### **The app crashed**
Please send an email with:
- Your iOS version  
- Device model  
- What you were doing when the crash happened  

This helps me debug quickly.
